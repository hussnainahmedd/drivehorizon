## 3. Current gameplay

Free-roam city driving and a repeatable delivery career: accept at dispatch, follow navigation through traffic, park and deliver, earn cash/XP, progress through ranks, maintain fuel/condition and fit upgrades. Late/damaged cargo still pays; roadside assistance works at zero balance. Maps, three cameras, headlamps, horn, day/night settings, graphics modes, audio, HUD, pause/help/settings, workshop and career journal are available. Progress and settings autosave.

The driving model is planar force-based bicycle dynamics with visual spring suspension. Traffic uses rounded closed road routes. These are intentional current gameplay implementations, not a full native rigid-body simulator or a citywide adaptive traffic network.

## 7. Current architecture

TypeScript/Three.js rendering, procedural geometry/textures, DOM/CSS HUD/menus, Web Audio, fixed-step simulation and browser-compatible storage. Vite compiles the application. Separate modules handle physics, world, traffic, progression/career, navigation, camera/recovery math, graphics negotiation, timing, interface and audio. Electron loads the same compiled assets directly via a local protocol; the renderer has an isolated, narrow platform bridge.
